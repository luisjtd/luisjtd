# Luis Torres

I’m a product manager from Nuevo León, Mexico. I started in backend engineering, and I still write software. A small example from Clínica Esquipulas probably says the most about how I work.

At the clinic, I design and build software for the people running it. One detail I cared about in the pharmacy workflow was what happened *after* someone opened a record. If they were halfway through a filtered inventory list, they should come back to that same list. And if they only wanted to look something up, opening the record should never put them into edit mode. Those are the kinds of product decisions I like being close enough to make and implement. [Clínica Esquipulas](https://clinicaesquipulas.com/streamlit/)

At **Coppel**, I lead product initiatives for WhatsApp and chat. Customers across Mexico use our WhatsApp channel to do things like request an account statement or start a loan application. A request that takes a customer one sentence can involve several systems and teams. My job is to work through those details with engineering and operations so the next step makes sense to the person holding the phone. [Coppel on WhatsApp](https://wa.me/5595000001)

It’s the kind of question that keeps me writing code alongside my product work. [Studio Racing on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/mx/app/studio-racing-by-rts/id6808104099)
